Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Gatherwell is a community companion with original reading guides, saved reads and a personal planner. Support: Jaytechxlab@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your saved guide IDs, plan titles and dates, completion status, theme choice and last valid community links are stored locally. No account is required. This version does not send your saved reads or personal plans to our servers and does not sync them across devices. Android backup is disabled in the supplied app configuration.

External connections

The app requests public WhatsApp and Telegram destinations from the App Link Manager hosted on Vercel. That service and its infrastructure receive connection information, including your IP address, as part of serving requests. A web host may process access logs when you use the website. No advertising or analytics SDK is included in this version.

Optional community services

Opening a WhatsApp or Telegram link takes you to an external service under its own privacy policy and terms. Those services may collect account and usage information. Joining a channel is optional; guides and planning do not require it.

Removing your data

Use About & privacy → Clear my saved data to delete this app’s local saved information. You can also clear browser site data or Android app storage. Local data is retained until you remove it or remove the app, subject to your device’s storage behaviour. There is no Gatherwell account to delete.

Permissions

The supplied Android app requests internet access to retrieve community settings and open external links. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions contact Jaytechxlab@gmail.com. This notice will be updated if app data handling changes.
